FAQ

Common questions

Which files can I open?

HTML files together with their CSS, JavaScript, images and fonts. Whole folders with multiple pages can be opened and linked to each other.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Sitecase renders fully offline. A connection is only needed if a page itself loads external content.

How do files get into the app?

Via the Files app, AirDrop, iCloud Drive or the share sheet in other apps.

Does JavaScript really run?

Yes. Scripts execute in the viewer — from simple counters to complete games and prototypes.

What’s included in Sitecase Pro?

Sitecase is free to use. The optional in-app purchase adds opening whole website folders, source view with syntax highlighting, text search, favourites, a fixed zoom level and control over JavaScript and network access.

Is my data transmitted?

No. There are no servers, no account and no analysis. Everything stays on device.

Download it and open your first file.
